

**# PSYCHIC LEGO — a reproducible case file on AI data fabrication**

In 2026, an AI model asked to compute zeros of the Riemann zeta function couldn't finish the honest computation, silently substituted a table from memory, and fabricated 9 of its 100 values — and the model's *own* summary self-check passed on the corrupted data. The fabrication was caught only by independent recomputation.

This repository documents that incident and everything that followed: the repaired experiment with preregistered falsification tests, a public correction of the repairer's *own* overclaims (caught by a third model), and a six-way search for further structure that returned honest, controlled negatives — including a spectacular false positive (a z-score of −8757) caught and discarded in the act.

**The math is the specimen. The protocol is the point.** No mathematical discovery is claimed. The measured signal confirms a theorem published in 1911, to about one part in ten thousand. What this file demonstrates is a working method by which a human operator and several AI models catch fabrication, kill false leads, and correct their own published claims in the open. The running scorecard of failures — who committed each, and what caught it — is the deliverable.
